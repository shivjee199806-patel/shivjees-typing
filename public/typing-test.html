Understand your practice result

Gross WPM uses typed characters divided by five and elapsed minutes. Accuracy in this introductory test is the percentage of typed words that exactly match the corresponding passage words. Mistakes show typed words that do not match. Punctuation and spacing matter. This practice score is not an official recruitment score, and different exams may use different evaluation rules.
