JP Typing Referral System

Added on top of SEO + Share Result build.
- Each logged-in user gets a unique referral code/link.
- ?ref=CODE is saved in the visitor browser until candidate signup.
- Referral is recorded only after verified candidate account creation.
- Candidate Dashboard shows referral link, signup count and paid conversions.
- WhatsApp share + Copy Link buttons included.
- Owner sidebar: Referrals.
- Owner Referral Analytics: total referral signups, active referrers, approved paid conversions, top referrers, recent referral signups.
- Existing login/payment/typing behavior intentionally preserved.
