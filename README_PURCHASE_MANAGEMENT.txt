Shivjee's Typing - Purchase / Payment Management

Owner > Payments now shows:
- Candidate name, email, phone
- Exam purchased
- Paid amount
- Purchase date
- Valid till date
- Remaining validity days
- Transaction/UTR
- Payment status

For approved purchases Owner can change:
- Paid amount
- Validity days

Saving validity recalculates the candidate's exam access from today and updates valid_until.
This changes only that candidate's purchase/access; it does not change the exam's default fee/validity.
